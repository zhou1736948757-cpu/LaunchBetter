## Addendum (2026-08-26, T-014): B1 evidence scope clarification

The B1 test `startSettleReplacementKeepsTelemetrySessionsIsolated`
(`:1516-1609`) is correctly named for what it proves; this addendum tightens
the evidence-scope wording. Its Grid E2E path is:

second real `pagingProbeGesture` → `beginGesture()` interrupts the in-flight
settle → phase tracking → `endGesture()` → `startSettle()`.

Therefore the Grid E2E test verifies **telemetry-session isolation after a
real gesture interruption**. It does **not** verify "the Grid E2E directly
enters the `startSettle(.settling)` replacement branch". The direct
`startSettle(.settling)` replacement lifecycle is covered by controller-level
tests. Together the two cover the complete evidence:

- Grid E2E (`startSettleReplacementKeepsTelemetrySessionsIsolated`): real
  gesture interruption → session isolation, end-to-end;
- controller-level test: direct `startSettle(.settling)` replacement
  lifecycle.

Do not phrase the Grid E2E test as directly entering the
`startSettle(.settling)` branch. The original body above is unmodified.
