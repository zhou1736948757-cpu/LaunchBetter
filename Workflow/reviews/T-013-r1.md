clarification, appended record-only after T-014 review of the B1 evidence
wording:

(`PageCompositorGridIntegrationTests.swift:1516-1609`) is: second real
`pagingProbeGesture` → `beginGesture()` interrupts the in-flight settle →

**not** directly enter the `startSettle(.settling)` replacement branch at the
Grid E2E level. The direct `startSettle(.settling)` replacement lifecycle is
covered by controller-level tests. Together the two constitute the complete
evidence:

- Grid E2E: real gesture interruption → session isolation, end-to-end;
- controller-level test: direct `startSettle(.settling)` replacement
  lifecycle.

Item 12's wording ("real second gesture as the replacement entry; Grid E2E
test proves session isolation and delivery order end-to-end") is consistent
with this clarification and is kept as-is. The original review body above is
